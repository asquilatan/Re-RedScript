## 2024-05-22 - Geometry Rasterization Optimization
**Learning:** For rasterizing geometric shapes (like spheres), iterating over the bounding box and checking `dist_sq <= r^2` is significantly slower ($O(R^3)$) than calculating exact loop bounds ($O(R^2)$). Specifically, for hollow shapes, calculating the valid "shell" segments avoids iterating the inner volume entirely, yielding massive speedups (e.g., ~69x for R=100 sphere).
**Action:** When implementing rasterization for other shapes (ellipsoids, cones), always derive the exact loop bounds and exclusion logic instead of using bounding box filters.
